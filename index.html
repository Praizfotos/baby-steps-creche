STEP 01 Who we are

Familiar faces, every single day

Baby Steps is a 12-place creche in a converted corner house on Kastanienallee. One group of infants, one of toddlers, one of pre-schoolers — and the same caregivers with them from Monday to Friday.

We opened it because we wanted somewhere we would happily leave our own children: quiet when it needs to be loud, unhurried, and run by people who notice when a child is off their food before the parent does.

01

One key person per child

Your child is paired with a named caregiver on day one. The same face at drop-off, the same face at pick-up, and someone who already knows how your child likes to be held.

02

Outside, in every weather

Two hours a day in our garden or across the road in Volkspark Prenzlauer Berg. Rain suits and spare boots live by the door — we would rather you send a puddle suit than stay home.

03

Food cooked here, this morning

Organic, mostly vegetarian, eaten together at the table. Menus go out every Sunday, and we cook around allergies rather than around convenience.

STEP 02 Our programs

Three rooms, three rhythms

Children move up when they are ready, not when the calendar says so. Each room has its own rhythm, its own nap window, and its own two caregivers.

6 – 18 months

Little Sprouts

Feeding and nap times follow the schedule you already have at home, so the first weeks are about trust rather than adjustment. Lots of floor time, water play, and one adult who stays with the same small group all day.

  • ✓ Home nap and feeding rhythm, kept for at least three months
  • ✓ Sensory corner, mirror wall and low climbing frames
  • ✓ Nappy changes logged and shared in the daily note
See the daily rhythm
18 months – 3 years

Toddler Trailblazers

The age of “me do it”. We build the day around short, repeatable tasks — hanging coats, setting cups, putting shoes in the basket — and give toddlers the time to finish them.

  • ✓ Circle time, songs and first words in German and English
  • ✓ Potty training supported at your pace, not ours
3 – 5 years

Pre-K Pathfinders

Letters, numbers and long projects — building a shop, growing cress, posting letters to the post office on Schönhauser Allee. School readiness comes out of real tasks, not worksheets.

  • ✓ Weekly project weeks with a walk into the neighbourhood
  • ✓ A transition plan with your Grundschule from the spring
“ Children don't need to be rushed. They need the same face, the same garden, and enough time to finish what they started.
Nadine Krause — lead educator, Baby Steps
STEP 03 A day here

A day your child can predict

Rhythm beats variety. When children know what happens next, they stop testing the day and start enjoying it.

07:30 – 09:00

Arrival & free play

Slow start. Coats off, breakfast at the big table, and whatever is out on the floor that morning.

09:00 – 10:00

Morning circle

Songs, the weather, and one long activity — painting, water play, or the week's project.

10:00 – 12:00

Outside, whatever the weather

Our garden in summer, Volkspark in the rain. Buckets, mud kitchen, bikes and the long walk to the duck pond.

12:00 – 13:00

Lunch together

Warm food cooked on site, served family style. Everyone sits, nobody is rushed, seconds if you want them.

13:00 – 15:00

Rest & quiet corners

Cots for the little ones; books, puzzles and drawing mats for those who have stopped sleeping.

15:00 – 17:00

Snack, photo, pick-up

Fruit and bread at 15:00. Your update lands at 15:00 too. Late pick-up until 17:00, by arrangement.

Sample day for the toddler room. Infant and pre-school rooms shift by about an hour either way.

Good to know

Questions we get asked first

From 6 months to 5 years, in three rooms: Little Sprouts (6–18 months), Toddler Trailblazers (18 months–3 years) and Pre-K Pathfinders (3–5 years). Children move up when they are ready, usually over two or three weeks rather than on one fixed date.

Monday to Friday, 07:30 to 17:00. Core hours are 09:00–15:00, when all groups run the same programme; arrival and pick-up are flexible around that. Late pick-up after 16:30 is included, and we ask for a phone call if you expect to be after 17:00.

Yes. Organic breakfast from 07:30, a warm lunch cooked on site, and two snacks — one mid-morning, one at 15:00. Menus go out every Sunday. Vegetarian is our default, and we cook around allergies and intolerances rather than around what is easy. Water and milk are available all day.

Two to three weeks, at your child's pace. On day one you stay for the whole morning. We then shorten your visits together — an hour, then two, then lunch without you — and your key caregiver calls you the moment your child settles, not at the end of the day. We do not run a fixed 30-day programme.

Please do. Tours run Tuesday and Thursday at 10:00, when the rooms are in full swing and you can see an ordinary day rather than a tidy one. Book by phone or through the form below — we keep group tours to three families at a time.

We accept the Berlin Kita-Gutschein. Once the Bezirksamt has issued it, bring the voucher to your tour and we handle the paperwork. Meals are billed separately at €68 per month. If you are waiting on a voucher, talk to us — we hold places rather than lose them over paperwork.

Something we have not covered? Ask us directly — a person replies, usually the same day.
